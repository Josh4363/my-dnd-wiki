This refers to a point in time near the end of the First Great Era from 505-521, where there was a massive jewel rush in the [[The Jewel Desert]]. Before then it was quite rare for one to willingly enter that Desert as it is quite treacherous, but soon traders from the Desert kept coming in and trading with an immense amounts of jewels like diamonds, sapphires, rubies and emeralds. This, alongside a story of a warrior in a full suit of diamond, sparked a grand race into the [[The Jewel Desert]] searching for these jewels and mining them.

Despite some level of success that a select few prospectors had, this event was deemed untrue and a hoax for the most part. Many mortals left their homes in search of this new life mining in the desert. According to a census taken in Nuva Prima, in 1st-E-514y, during the height of the rush 18,000 residents reported moving out of the hub City in search for fortune.
